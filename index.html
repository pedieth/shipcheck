What I check

  • Row level securityEvery table: is RLS on, and do the policies actually stop one user reading another's rows?
  • Leaked keysService role keys, admin SDK credentials or third-party secrets shipped to the browser.
  • StoragePublic buckets, listable files, uploads anyone can overwrite.
  • Auth settingsOpen sign-ups where they shouldn't be, email confirmation, redirect URLs.
  • Edge functions and RPCFunctions callable without auth, or trusting a user id sent from the client.
  • Firebase rulesFirestore, Realtime Database and Storage rules that allow read or write to everyone.

Questions

Do you need access to my database?

No. Quick Check works from the outside, the same way an attacker would. Audit + Fix is easier with read-only dashboard access, but it's optional.

What if you find nothing?

You get a report that says what was checked and that it passed. That's worth having before launch or a fundraise.

Is this a full penetration test?

No. It's a focused check of the misconfigurations that most often expose data in Supabase and Firebase apps. It doesn't guarantee your app is secure in every way.

Why no calls?

Everything is written so you have a record, and so you can forward it to your team.
